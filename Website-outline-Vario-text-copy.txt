Our Mission
Retirement should be safe, predictable, and built on more than the hope that the market will cooperate. We help retirees protect their future with smarter planning and access to alternative investment strategies usually unseen in traditional advisory relationships.

Our Story
My name is Zach Christensen, and the roots of Vario Advisors go back to my grandparents in Victor, Idaho.
My grandfather trusted the stock market with his retirement savings. When the market turned, everything he worked for was wiped out. To make ends meet, he took a job in a nursing home — working until the final days of his life.
My grandmother’s story was different. She owned land — a real, tangible asset. When she sold it, that one decision provided the financial security that the stock market couldn’t.
These two outcomes shaped my life and my philosophy.
Real assets matter. Predictability matters. Retirees deserve better.
Vario Advisors was created with a mission: to help families build retirements that aren’t dependent on the stock market.

What Is an Accredited Investor?
Accredited investors have access to private-market investment opportunities that are not available to the general public. Many retirees qualify based on their real estate equity, investment accounts, or overall net worth — even if they’ve never considered themselves “wealthy.”
